Welcome to Royal Delights

Located along the vibrant routes of Rajahmundry, Royal Delights brings you the deep smoky complexity of traditional North-Indian methods paired seamlessly with fiery local Andhra signatures.

Our kitchen hosts custom designed clay ovens fired continuously to provide that absolute perfect golden char on every piece of meat and artisan flatbread arriving at your table.
